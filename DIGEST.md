She begins engaging in criminal activities in Liberty City, Liberty State and Vice City, Leonida, but is subsequently arrested and placed into Leonida Penitentiary [3]. She is later released on parole with an ankle monitor [3]. Lucia was designed to be an absolute independent, stoic, and strong female protagonist, intended to avoid common clichés [3].

## 1Password Security Incident — what is this about and why does it matter?

1Password is a password manager developed by AgileBits Inc [1]. that stores sensitive information in an encrypted vault locked with a PBKDF2-guarded master password, with vaults hosted on 1Password's servers for a monthly fee [1]. Unlike Todoist, which recently suffered a security incident involving unauthorized access to customer data including password hashes [2], 1Password has not been associated with a recent security breach in the provided results. LastPass, by contrast, suffered significant security incidents between 2011 and 2022, including a 2022 breach where user data, billing information, and vaults were compromised [3]. Credential stuffing attacks exploit password reuse, with 81% of users reusing passwords across multiple sites [4], making password managers like 1Password valuable for generating and storing unique credentials. Apple also offers a password manager available for iOS 18, iPadOS 18, macOS Sequoia, and visionOS 2 or higher [6]. The Todoist incident matters because an attacker used a stolen development credential to access production systems and retrieve customer information including email addresses and password hashes, prompting Todoist to reset affected passwords and enforce tighter access controls [2].
