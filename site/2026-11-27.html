Manufacturer reliability varies significantly 4. Based on Backblaze dataset analysis, HGST drives show the lowest failure rates—about 41% of Seagate's 4. Price tracking matters because HDDs remain dominant secondary storage despite solid-state alternatives 36. Historically, IBM introduced the first HDD in 1956 37, and the technology has evolved from data-center-only devices to ubiquitous consumer storage. Modern HDDs store data on rigid platters with magnetic heads, maintaining random-access capabilities while offering non-volatile retention 3.

Stable Gated DeltaNet-2 (SGDN-2) proposes symmetric erasure to ensure non-expansive state propagation and decay-regulated residual writing to preserve writing flexibility 1. This resolves training failures in long-context training caused by asymmetric reading and modification directions during erasure, which previously led to exponential state growth even without new writes 1. Theoretically, the method establishes an $O(\sqrt{T})$ upper bound on recurrent state growth while retaining chunkwise parallel computation 1. Experiments on ~1.6B parameter models show SGDN-2 achieves stable training alongside improved long-context retrieval, boosting the 128K average RULER score by 6.80 points in the recurrent setting and average accuracy across nine downstream tasks by 0.62 points in the hybrid setting compared to GDN-2 1.

OS-Omni proposes a live cross-platform benchmark and execution environment for evaluating computer-using agents across Windows, Ubuntu, macOS, iOS, Android, and Web environments 12. It matters because existing benchmarks typically isolate single platforms, leaving unclear whether a single generalist agent can execute long-horizon workflows spanning multiple operating systems and persistent application states 12. The benchmark releases tasks in versioned waves of 300 each; v1 contains 48 tasks per platform and 12 cross-platform workflows pairing a desktop with an Android phone 12. OS-Omni provides reproducible initialization, platform-specific adapters, trajectory logging, executable final-state evaluators, and controlled service-backed applications for workflows involving account, cart, budget, calendar, and message states 12. Human studies show OS-Omni tasks require 67.54 interaction steps, substantially exceeding OSWorld references of 15.0 steps 12. On v1, the strongest evaluated agent, Claude Opus 5.5, reaches 68.5% average success, trailing the 77.3% human reference by 8.8 points; the gap is uneven across platforms, with frontier models exceeding humans on Linux but trailing them by more than 25 points on iOS and macOS 12. These results indicate that current agents remain far from platform-robust, long-horizon computer use, especially when success requires sustained state tracking and durable final-state changes 12.
