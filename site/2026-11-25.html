The Spray Bottle — what is this about and why does it matter?

The Spray Bottle refers to a functional manipulation task where robots must learn to aim and actuate a spray bottle to apply its function (e.g., watering a plant) to a target 2. It matters because it requires integrating semantic understanding of the object's function, precise pose control, and dexterous actuation to achieve stable grasp and effective application 2.

Key points:

  • CoDex is a zero-demonstration framework that discovers spray-bottle manipulation strategies using vision-language models to infer semantic constraints, which guide analytic optimization and reinforcement learning to generate transferable grasp-move-actuate policies 2.
  • The task involves previously unseen objects with internal mechanisms, showcasing the system's ability to execute physically viable dexterous behaviors without human demonstrations 2.
  • Spray bottles are one of several CD-FOM tasks (alongside hot glue guns, air dusters, flashlights, and pepper grinders) that require actuating an object's internal mechanism while controlling its pose 2.
